Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 August 2026  ·  Last updated: 16 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pace Lodgix (“Pace Lodgix,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you use our property and booking management platform (the “Service”), our website at pacelodgix.com, and any related pages, including public guest booking pages served by our customers.

Pace Lodgix is operated by Charlene Pace, a sole proprietor based in Sta. Rosa City, Laguna, Philippines. We are committed to handling personal information responsibly and in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

1Our two roles & who this covers

Pace Lodgix acts in two different capacities, and it matters which one applies to you:

  • As a data controller — for information about the property owners, managers, and staff who register for and use the Service (our “Customers”), and for visitors to our marketing website. Here we decide how and why the data is processed, and this policy governs that processing.
  • As a data processor — for the guest and booking information that our Customers enter into, or receive through, their own accounts (“Customer Data”). In this case the Customer is the data controller; we process that information only on their behalf and under their instructions. If you are a guest who booked a stay, the business you booked with is responsible for your information — please see Section 5.

2Information we collect

a. Information you provide directly

  • Account & profile: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), and the role you hold within a business.
  • Business information: your business name, logo, contact details, currency, tax settings, policies, social links, and other configuration you add in Settings.
  • Demo & contact requests: the name, email, business name, and number of units you submit through our request or contact forms.
  • Support communications: the contents of messages you send us.

b. Customer Data you enter into the Service

In the ordinary course of using Pace Lodgix, our Customers store operational data such as property details and rates, reservations, guest names and contact details, payment amounts and status, invoices, ledger entries, and calendar information imported from third-party platforms. We process this data as a processor on the Customer's behalf (see Section 1).

c. Information collected automatically

  • Technical & usage data: IP address, browser type, device and operating system, pages viewed, and timestamps — used to operate, secure, and improve the Service.
  • Local storage: your login session (JWT) and your appearance preference (Light / Night Dim / Auto) are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. See Section 6.
  • Audit logs: actions taken within an account (who did what, and when) are recorded to an activity log for security and accountability.

3How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and secure the Service and your account;
  • Authenticate users and enforce access controls and tenant isolation;
  • Send transactional and service messages (e.g. account, security, and booking-related notifications);
  • Respond to your demo requests, enquiries, and support tickets;
  • Monitor, troubleshoot, and improve performance, reliability, and features;
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents; and
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use Customer Data to build advertising profiles.

4Legal bases for processing

Under the Data Privacy Act, we rely on one or more of the following bases, depending on the context:

  • Contract — to provide the Service you signed up for;
  • Consent — where you have given it, such as for certain communications (which you may withdraw at any time);
  • Legitimate interests — to secure, maintain, and improve the Service, provided these do not override your rights; and
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by law.

5Guest data & our customers

If you are a guest and you submitted a booking through a public booking page (for example, a link ending in book.html?tenant=…), the personal information you provided — such as your name, contact details, and stay dates — is collected by the business you booked with, not by Pace Lodgix directly. That business is the data controller for your information.

  • To exercise your privacy rights over your booking information, please contact the business you booked with.
  • Pace Lodgix processes that data only to provide the platform to that business, and under our agreement with them.
  • If you are unsure who to contact, you may reach us at privacy@pacelodgix.com and we will help route your request to the responsible business.

6Cookies & local storage

Pace Lodgix uses a minimal set of browser storage mechanisms, focused on making the Service work rather than on tracking:

  • Strictly necessary: your authenticated session, stored in local storage, keeps you signed in.
  • Preferences: your appearance mode (Light / Night Dim / Auto), and whether you have dismissed the beta notice on our website, are stored per device and are not tied to your identity.
  • Advertising: we do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, retargeting tags, or cross-site advertising tools on this website or in the Service. We do not track you across other websites.
  • Visitor measurement: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics on our marketing website to count page views and see which pages and referring links are useful. It is cookie-free and stores nothing on your device: no cookie, no local storage entry, no identifier, and no fingerprinting. It does not follow you to other websites and cannot be used to identify you, which is why it is not subject to a consent banner.
  • Content delivery: our marketing website loads an animation library (GSAP) from Cloudflare's public content delivery network. As part of delivering that file, Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser type. It is not used to identify or profile you. Our fonts are served from our own domain and are not requested from any third party.

You can clear this storage through your browser at any time, though doing so will sign you out and reset your preferences.

7How we share information

We share personal information only in these circumstances:

  • Service providers (sub-processors) who host and support the Service on our behalf — see Section 8;
  • Within your business — team members you invite can see shared account data according to their role;
  • Legal & safety — where required by law, regulation, legal process, or a valid government request, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Pace Lodgix, our users, or the public;
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy; and
  • With your direction or consent — for example, when you connect a third-party calendar (Airbnb, Booking.com, etc.).

8Service providers

We rely on a small number of trusted infrastructure providers to run the Service. Each processes data only as needed to provide its service to us:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and backend hosting.
  • Cloudflare — website and application hosting, content delivery, and security.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery, for sending booking confirmations, invoices, team invitations, and account emails on our behalf.
  • Calendar platforms — Airbnb, Booking.com, and similar services, where a Customer chooses to connect them via iCal.

Some of these providers may process or store data outside the Philippines — see Section 11.

9Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account and Customer Data is retained for the life of your account and for a reasonable period afterward, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or the law requires longer retention (for example, tax and accounting records).
  • Demo and contact form submissions are retained for as long as needed to respond and follow up.
  • Audit logs are retained to support security and accountability.

When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it. Note that some deletions within the Service are “soft” deletes retained for audit purposes before permanent removal.

10How we protect data

We apply organisational and technical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data, including:

  • Row-Level Security in the database, so each business's data is isolated and every query is checked server-side;
  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and passwords stored only as secure hashes;
  • Role-based access controls and audit logging within accounts; and
  • Reliance on reputable, security-focused infrastructure providers.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to give rise to serious harm, we will notify the National Privacy Commission and affected individuals as required by law.

11International transfers

Because some of our service providers operate globally, your information may be processed or stored in countries other than the Philippines. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure your information continues to be protected consistent with the Data Privacy Act, including through the contractual and security commitments of our providers.

12Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, and in particular the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal data is processed;
  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated data;
  • Object to processing, or request erasure or blocking where permitted;
  • Data portability — to obtain a copy of certain data in a usable format;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • Lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 15. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. If your request relates to booking data held by a business you booked with, please see Section 5.

13Children

The Service is intended for businesses and adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above, and, for material changes, provide a more prominent notice. Your continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
