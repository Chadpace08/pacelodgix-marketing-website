Calendar sync

Your calendars stay in step. Your books stay yours.

Connect Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda, or other platforms that provide a calendar link. Bookings and blocked dates stay in sync across connected calendars.

What arrives on its own

Pace Lodgix syncs reservation dates and availability from your connected calendars. Guest details and payment amounts aren't included, and removed reservations update automatically.

What you fill in

Calendar feeds do not include payout amounts, so you enter the amount you received and keep the payment record with the booking.

What it will not touch

If a synced booking already has a payment recorded against it, Pace Lodgix protects that record and tells you why it was not changed.

Sync availability through calendar feeds, no direct integration or approval required. Full reservation-data integrations are not currently supported.

Deposits and payments

Every payment. One clear record.

Record payment details and proof directly on the booking, so everything stays together.

FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

What is a property management system (PMS)?

A property management system is software that runs the day-to-day operations of a rental business from one place: reservations, the booking calendar, guest records, invoices, payments and financial reports. For vacation rentals and short-term rentals, a PMS like Pace Lodgix replaces the spreadsheets and message threads most hosts start out with, so availability and pricing stay correct across every booking platform.

Is Pace Lodgix good for Airbnb hosts and short-term rentals?

Yes. Pace Lodgix is vacation rental software built for Airbnb hosts, short-term rental operators, staycations, villas, apartments and beach resorts. It works alongside the platforms you already list on, syncing your calendar both ways so the same dates are never sold twice, and gives you a commission-free direct booking page of your own.

How much does Pace Lodgix vacation rental software cost?

Plans start at ₱399 a month for 1-5 properties and ₱599 a month for 6-15, with custom pricing for 16 or more. Every plan includes the full platform, with no feature paywalls, you pay for the number of properties you run. Each plan starts with a 14-day free trial and no card is required.

A yearly payment covers 12 months and the first payment is refundable within 14 days. For monthly billing, contact us and we'll set it up.

Do I have to stop using Airbnb or Booking.com?

No. Pace Lodgix syncs your calendar with them. Connect each platform's calendar link and availability stays in step both ways. A booking on Airbnb blocks the dates here, and a direct booking here blocks them there.

Does a booking from Airbnb arrive with the guest name and the amount?

Partly, and it depends on the platform. Connecting a calendar link brings the reservation and its dates across on its own and blocks the night here, tagged with the platform it came from. Some sites publish the guest name in that feed and it arrives with it; Airbnb usually does not. No platform publishes the payout in a calendar link, so you enter what you were paid. That takes a moment per booking and keeps your records under your control.

Can guests book directly, without a platform commission?

Yes. That is the point of your booking page. Every business gets its own link. A guest picks dates, sees the live price, and submits. It arrives as Pending and nothing is confirmed until you approve it.

Can my guests pay by card on my booking page?

Not yet. Today a guest pays by GCash, bank transfer or cash and attaches the proof on the booking form, and you confirm it in one tap. Online card and e-wallet payment is in build. When it arrives you will connect your own payment account, so the money goes straight to you and Pace Lodgix never holds it or takes a share of a booking.

Does it handle weekend, holiday and long-stay pricing?

All of it, plus per-guest-count brackets, cleaning fees, extra-head charges, automatic length-of-stay discounts and platform commission. You set the rules once and the price computes itself on every booking.

Can my staff use it without seeing everything?

Yes. Invite your team as owner, manager or staff. Deletion is owner-gated and every change lands in the activity log with a name and a timestamp.

Is my data separated from other businesses?

Yes, enforced in the database itself, not only in the app. Every row is scoped to your business and the security policy is checked on every single query.

What does onboarding look like?

Hands-on. We set your properties, rates and channels up with you, so the first thing you see is your own business, not an empty screen.
